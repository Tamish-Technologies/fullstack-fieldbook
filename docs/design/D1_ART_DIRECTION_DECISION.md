# D1 Art-Direction Decision — FullStack Fieldbook

**Record:** A-015 / D1-ADR-001

**Owner selection:** **A — Technical Editorial**  
**Scope:** Art direction only. **D1 NOT fully approved. D2 NOT authorized.**

## What the owner chose

FullStack Fieldbook should look and feel like a meticulously designed **technical field guide with an interactive coding desk**. The application is a self-paced textbook + lab, not a game, social platform or analytics product. A learner should immediately understand what they are studying, how they will apply it and what evidence unlocks the next chapter.

## Binding art-direction traits

1. **Reading comes first:** beautiful lesson typography, meaningful hierarchy, well-sized prose column and clear progress context.
2. **Precise editorial details:** structured chapter numbers, restrained borders, annotations and lesson outlines where they help comprehension.
3. **Practical coding surface:** separate editor, output/test panel, and explainable assessment feedback; usability over decoration.
4. **Calm but credible:** neutral surfaces with restrained indigo brand emphasis, teal only for verified progress, amber for hints, and explicit error states. Exact palette remains subject to screen-level contrast review.
5. **Subtle progress navigation:** include resume, locked prerequisites and week completion without turning the home page into a chart-heavy dashboard.
6. **Accessibility:** keyboard focus, semantic contrast, mobile reading, dark mode and reduced motion must be demonstrated in designs.
7. **Truthful evidence:** automated checks and self-verified local projects must look distinctly labeled. No certificate-style claims that imply independent validation.

## Not selected

The earlier **B — Calm Learning Workspace** was not selected as the overall style. Its *clear progress organization* can inform usability, but should not replace Technical Editorial's reading-first identity. No additional style vote is required unless the owner requests one.
