## Positioning

A self-paced, hands-on full-stack development fieldbook: learners read clear explanations, work through interactive examples, prove understanding at checkpoints, and build progressively larger real applications. The platform itself runs locally in the browser with no app backend, account, or paid AI API requirement.

## Voice and design

- Credible, welcoming, precise, direct, practice-first.
- Visual direction under review: a modern technical fieldbook, annotated examples, calm educational UI, indigo/teal palette, and comfortable long-form reading.
- **Optional tagline for review (not yet approved):** “Your practical guide to building real applications.”
- Avoid implying certificates, guaranteed careers, or that full-stack backend exercises run entirely in the browser.

## Important boundaries

- Renaming the brand must **not** rename stable course, lesson, checkpoint or test IDs.
- There is no app yet; do not create a migration or claim deployed users have stored progress.
- The original twelve instructor PDFs, Markdown guides, and reference-code archives are preserved without modification.
- Domain availability, trademarks, logo licensing, and public release rights have **not** been verified. Check them before publishing.
- Do not infer permission to code; continue D0/D1 planning until explicit owner approval of D2.
